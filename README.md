# "Love Like a Rocket" LED Chaser

<!-- Describe your board in 2-3 sentences. What are you making? What will it do? -->

This PCB will flash 10 LEDs in sequence. It requires a 9V battery to power it. The board is designed to resemble a
flying rocket.

<!-- How much is it going to cost? -->

The total cost is roughly $50 CAD.
